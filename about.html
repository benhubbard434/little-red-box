Welcome to Little Red Box — a family-built business with a passion for timeless antique, retro, and vintage jewellery.

With over 1,000 carefully curated pieces in stock, we specialise in jewellery with character, history, and individuality. From elegant antique treasures to bold retro statement pieces and much-loved vintage classics, every item is personally selected for its quality and charm.

Little Red Box is built on more than 30 years of industry experience. Originally founded as JAM Jewellery by Jane and Mike, the business has grown through a genuine love of beautiful jewellery and the relationships built with customers over the years. Today, their daughter Charlotte proudly continues that legacy, bringing a fresh chapter to the business while keeping the same personal approach at its heart.

The name Little Red Box is inspired by the signature red jewellery boxes that every purchase is presented in — a small detail that has become a favourite among loyal customers from around the world.

We believe buying vintage and antique jewellery should feel personal, enjoyable, and never intimidating. Whether you are searching for a meaningful gift, a future heirloom, or simply an addition to your own collection, we are always happy to help.

If you cannot find exactly what you are looking for, we offer a complimentary Personal Shopper Service. Whether you have a specific piece in mind or do not know where to begin, we are always delighted to help guide your search using our years of expertise and industry contacts.

You can explore our collection online here or visit us at Hemswell Antique Centres, where we proudly display in Cabinets D8 & D9 in Building One, and Cabinets 7 & 8 in the Guardroom.

We look forward to helping you find something truly special.
